# Customer portal and customer documents

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Confirm the requesting operator, customer/recipient, document or job, and branch.

1. Retrieve authorized job/document state, linked customer, and existing approved sharing URL when the record returns one. Verify the intended recipient and whether the document is appropriate for external sharing. Keep other customers and private notes out of the response.
2. If the request includes portal access, invitation, public permissions or a newly generated link, use a dedicated verified tool or prepare a handoff in FieldCamp's Customer Portal or document view. Do not construct links from internal IDs.
3. Follow [estimate sending](../quote-a-job/SKILL.md) or [overdue follow-up](../chase-overdue/SKILL.md) for a document send. Use FieldCamp's approved in-app payment flow for payments, refunds or credits. This skill can summarize the payment status.
4. Return the verifiable customer-facing record state, action performed or prepared, and any pending access/configuration item.
