# Workspace administration

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md) and [setup](../setup/SKILL.md).

1. Verify the active workspace, division and administrator's scope. Record the specific outcome and report which settings or modules can be read. Workspaces, departments and branches can have different IDs and permissions.
2. Prepare a settings checklist for applicable business hours, service area, assigned roles, templates, billing defaults, data ownership and integrations. For migration or bulk import, prepare field mappings, duplicate handling, required fields and a small dry-run sample. Avoid copying production exports into an unrelated service.
3. Use a dedicated authorized tool only when it can express the requested change and return a verified configuration. Otherwise provide the precise in-app setting, intended value and owner. Account billing, plan changes and cross-workspace data moves require the responsible FieldCamp administrator.
4. Verify the result and its branch scope through a supported read or an observed in-app record. Use [setup](../setup/SKILL.md) to refresh workflow readiness after a workspace or permission change.
