# Accounting and integrations

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Confirm the account, integration, synchronization direction, period, branch and currency.

1. Read relevant FieldCamp documents, payments, clients and dates through scoped tools. Read the matched external record only when the operator has a supported authorized connection for that system and intends that access. Verify record pairing by external ID or other reliable reference.
2. Compare statuses, totals, taxes, currencies, last sync and whether a record exists on both sides. Classify missing, duplicated, amount mismatch, sync failed or unknown. An event in one system does not establish synchronization.
3. Prepare a sync or correction plan with source of truth, exact records, action, mapping and operator. Use authorized FieldCamp integration screens for OAuth setup, credentials, retries, mappings and exports when no dedicated tool exists. Never share credentials or re-create accounting records just to repair a failed sync.
4. Verify the original and destination after an approved sync, if both are accessible. Preserve uncertainty for external records you cannot confirm.
