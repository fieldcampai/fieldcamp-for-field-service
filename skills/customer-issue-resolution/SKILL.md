# Resolve a customer issue

Read [workflow execution](../../shared/run-a-workflow.md) and [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md).

1. Match the customer and site. Use [client brief](../client-brief/SKILL.md), approved [Customer Inbox](../customer-inbox/SKILL.md) reads when exposed, and job/visit history. Identify the reported issue, promises made, original scope and open case or follow-up.
2. Separate customer-reported facts, FieldCamp records and operator decisions. Determine whether next action is an existing service follow-up, warranty callback, new work proposal, billing review, or escalation. Apply [change orders and callbacks](../change-orders-and-callbacks/SKILL.md) where applicable.
3. Draft a resolution with timing, accountable owner and exact customer communication. Use [book a job](../book-a-job/SKILL.md) or [follow-ups](../follow-ups/SKILL.md) only after approval. Route credits/refunds to the appropriate FieldCamp or accounting workflow.
4. Record verifiable commitments. A draft, send acceptance, resolved conversation and completed service describe different milestones. Carry forward the linked record and what remains for a human to confirm.

Output the verified issue context, approved or proposed resolution, current customer communication status and the next follow-up.
