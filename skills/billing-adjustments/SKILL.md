# Billing adjustments and disputes

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Confirm workspace, billing customer, document number, currency and the requested outcome.

1. Read the document, linked job/estimate and payment history. Verify existing credits, refunds, deposits and balance when available. Keep cash received, recorded payments, open receivables and requested adjustments distinct.
2. Identify whether the operator needs a draft correction, accounting correction, customer credit, refund, or a new invoice. Prepare a before/after calculation with source documents and an exact reason. Check whether a published or locked document requires the finance team.
3. Use `update_document` only for an approved supported edit to an eligible document. For credit, refund, payment collection or write-off without a verified eligible tool, prepare the exact in-app finance handoff. Do not initiate money movement or repurpose manual payment logging for reversals.
4. Verify the state in FieldCamp and any linked accounting system where accessible. Hand disputed work to the accountable owner and avoid duplicate communications.
