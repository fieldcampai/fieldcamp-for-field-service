# Equipment and fleet readiness

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Confirm branch, actual asset, operator/vehicle and requested service window.

1. Read equipment assets, assigned jobs, maintenance dates and location only via authorized FieldCamp records or a verified dedicated tool. Use [custom records](../custom-records/SKILL.md) for workspace equipment objects. Verify freshness and distinguish equipment assignment from technician availability.
2. Check readiness, qualification, overlaps and applicable maintenance or inspection evidence. Avoid presenting a location or ETA from a calendar. If readiness is uncertain, flag the visit as needing a dispatcher decision.
3. Preview equipment assignments or maintenance follow-up. Where a dedicated booking or fleet tool is absent, give the operator a precise FieldCamp handoff with asset ID, visit, local time, and required action.
4. Verify the actual asset allocation, status or reminder and give the next owner unresolved exceptions.
