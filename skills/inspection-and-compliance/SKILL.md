# Inspections and field evidence

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md), [custom records](../custom-records/SKILL.md), and [forms and documentation](../field-forms-and-documentation/SKILL.md).

1. Verify customer/site/asset, authorized technician, inspection type and source template or configured custom object. Find the latest matching inspection and next due date when recorded. Confirm the branch version of the form.
2. Capture observed measurements, units, timestamps, supporting files and authorized sign-off. Record a missing result as missing. Keep customer-provided information distinct from field measurements. Where a formal finding depends on a standard, use the source and operator's documented determination.
3. Preview the target record, evidence, exceptions and corrective follow-up. Save with the supported custom-record tool after approval or hand off to the actual FieldCamp form and evidence collection. Do not declare an inspection certified when review, signature or submission is pending.
4. Verify the outcome and plan reassessment or follow-up. Track renewal and customer-facing reports separately.
