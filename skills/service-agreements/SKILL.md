# Service agreements and entitlements

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Verify the agreement and exact service site or asset before assuming coverage.

1. Read the verified native agreement or existing workspace custom record if accessible. Confirm active dates, covered assets and work, billing cadence and remaining visits. Check whether an active agreement, recurring job, or separately sold service backs each entitlement.
2. Identify covered, excluded or unresolved work. Do not treat a recurring job as a signed plan or infer warranty or no-charge terms from a note. Use approved agreement terms and raise conflicts to the operator.
3. Draft the plan or change with scope, price, cadence, renewal, acceptance evidence and scheduling effect. Run supported changes only through an approved dedicated operation or give the agreement owner a FieldCamp handoff. Confirm quote and recurring work independently.
4. Use [maintenance renewal](../maintenance-renewal/SKILL.md) for a renewal and [recurring work](../recurring-work/SKILL.md) for approved appointments. Show active agreement status, next eligible visit and unverified obligations.
