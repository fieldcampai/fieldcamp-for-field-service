# Parts usage and job costing

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). Confirm branch, job, related visits and the commercial baseline being compared.

1. Identify the approved estimate and billed lines, actual time entries where available, unit costs, cost date, actual parts used, vendor charges and receipts. Separate estimates, stock availability, consumption and committed purchase quantities.
2. Compare actual and planned usage, reporting basis, variance and missing entries. Never infer actual cost from the retail price book or authorized amount. Keep internal cost and margin figures out of customer-facing documents.
3. Use a dedicated, authorized connector action for recording consumption or job costs when exposed. Otherwise prepare the approved job-log, inventory issue/return, or costing handoff. Use [stock operations](../restock/SKILL.md) for warehouse moves.
4. Verify linked cost or movement records. Report partial costing coverage and exceptions that block billing.
