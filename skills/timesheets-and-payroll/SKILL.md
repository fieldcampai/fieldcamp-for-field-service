# Timesheets and payroll

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md). These records can be sensitive. Confirm authorized staff scope, pay period, timezone and branch.

1. Use a dedicated authorized timekeeping tool if available. Otherwise request the approved timesheet export or point the operator to FieldCamp's Timesheets view. Use job and visit timestamps as scheduling context. They do not prove payable hours.
2. Report missing punches, overlapping entries, unassigned time, travel, breaks and relevant policies with separate columns for verified and disputed time. Do not invent a worker's entries or calculate payroll from a calendar. Overtime and deductions require the operator's configured rules and review.
3. Prepare a correction/export handoff with original record, proposed correction, reason, employee, period and reviewer. Confirm edits only through a dedicated permission-checked tool. Use the in-app process for locking time, payroll submission and export to the payroll provider.
4. Return the status of the reviewed period and unresolved exceptions. Avoid accessing bank or employee identity details beyond the task.
