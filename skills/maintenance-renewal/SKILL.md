# Maintenance and renewal

Read [workflow execution](../../shared/run-a-workflow.md) and [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md).

1. Resolve the customer, service sites, assets, agreement or configured recurring record, expiry date and existing jobs. Use [customer sites](../customer-sites-and-assets/SKILL.md) and [custom records](../custom-records/SKILL.md). If the agreement module is unavailable, prepare a manual renewal review from verified records and flag missing obligations.
2. Compare the existing terms, completed versus owed visits, site access and required staff qualifications. Draft a renewal scope and schedule, or flag missed entitlements before proposing new work.
3. Produce an estimate or agreement handoff through [quote a job](../quote-a-job/SKILL.md). Get explicit approval before saving or sending an offer, and wait for the documented acceptance required by the workspace.
4. Use [recurring work](../recurring-work/SKILL.md) for the approved cadence, occurrences and exceptions. Confirm the next visit, recurring parent, effective date and lifecycle of the old schedule. Never renew an agreement merely by creating a new job.
5. Verify the agreement and appointment state using observable FieldCamp records or label a FieldCamp handoff. Give the next owner a renewal or follow-up task proposal where needed.

Return the agreement or source references, approved scope, next due date, and pending customer or administrator decisions.
