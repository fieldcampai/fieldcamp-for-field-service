# Change orders and callbacks

Read [operating rules](../../shared/operating-rules.md).

1. Read the original job, accepted scope, latest visit, existing quote/invoice and related tasks. Determine whether the request is an approved-scope correction, potential warranty coverage, or new billable work. Flag conflicts and let the owner decide when coverage is unclear.
2. Draft the changed scope with old/new lines, value, service impact, open questions, and whether a prior estimate or invoice already exists. Use [quote a job](../quote-a-job/SKILL.md) to review any approved estimate. A change to an approved commercial record may require a new document or in-app procedure.
3. For a callback, use [booking](../book-a-job/SKILL.md) to propose a linked visit and crew. Verify whether the product permits that visit on the original job or requires a new linked job. Avoid a duplicate job or bill. For a claim resolution, use [customer issue resolution](../customer-issue-resolution/SKILL.md).
4. Preview each write, record the resulting IDs, and carry forward what is awaiting customer approval or field action. Route credits/refunds through the authorized in-app finance process.
